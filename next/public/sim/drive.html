What is being simulated?

NASA’s Curiosity model on a measured HiRISE elevation grid: 1.01 m per cell, with no exaggerated heights. The rendered surface and collision mesh use the same samples.

Gravity: 3.71 m/s². Mass: 899 kg. Commanded top speed: 4 cm/s. Six raycast wheels apply spring, traction, braking and steering forces to a rigid chassis.

This is an educational approximation. Independent springs stand in for the rocker-bogie linkage. Sub-metre rocks and soil deformation are not modeled. HiRISE orbital brightness is mapped to the measured terrain. Tint and fine grain are illustrative. Wind applies approximate drag in thin Martian air; dust changes lighting and visibility. Scenarios are not live weather; this is not a replay of rover telemetry or a NASA-validated dynamics model.
