THE ROVER BEHIND THE PHOTOS

This is Curiosity.

Curiosity is about the size of a car. It launched on November 26, 2011, and landed in Gale Crater on August 6, 2012, to study whether Mars once had conditions suitable for microbial life.

Its 17 cameras photograph the terrain, help the rover navigate, and inspect rocks up close. You can browse those images in the archive above.
